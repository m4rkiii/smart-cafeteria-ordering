# User Research Findings (Raw Data & Metrics)
## Smart Cafeteria Ordering Mobile Application

**Interview Period:** Week 1  
**Participants:** 5 Campus Representatives (3 Students, 2 Staff)  

---

### Participant Profile Matrix

| ID | Role | Department | Cafeteria Visit Frequency | Avg. Wait Time Reported | Primary Frustration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | Undergraduate (Yr 3) | Computer Science | 5x / week | 28 mins | Long queues & sold-out items |
| **P2** | Undergraduate (Yr 2) | Mechanical Eng. | 4x / week | 35 mins | Missing lectures due to line delays |
| **P3** | Postgraduate (Master's) | Business School | 3x / week | 20 mins | No time between job & lectures |
| **P4** | Associate Professor | Economics | 3x / week | 25 mins | Unpredictable lunch break timing |
| **P5** | Administrative Staff | Student Registry | 5x / week | 15 mins | No quiet pickup point |

---

### Quantitative Survey Results Summary (N = 5)

#### 1. Average Peak Wait Time
- **10–20 mins:** 20% (1 participant)
- **20–35 mins:** 60% (3 participants)
- **35+ mins:** 20% (1 participant)
- **Mean Wait Time:** ~24.6 minutes per visit.

#### 2. Likelihood to Adopt Mobile Pre-Ordering
- **Yes, definitely:** 80% (4 participants)
- **Probably:** 20% (1 participant)
- **Net Adoption Willingness:** 100% positive intent.

#### 3. Preferred Payment Method
- **M-Pesa Mobile Money:** 60% (3 participants)
- **Campus Smart Card Account:** 20% (1 participant)
- **Pay at Collection:** 20% (1 participant)

#### 4. Scheduled Pickup Slot Preference
- **Extremely Important:** 80% (4 participants)
- **Moderately Important:** 20% (1 participant)

---

### Qualitative Interview Quotes

> **P1 (CS Student):** *"The worst thing is standing in line for 20 minutes only to get to the counter and be told the beef stew is finished. If the app showed 'Sold Out' beforehand, I'd pick something else instantly."*

> **P2 (Eng. Student):** *"I have lab at 1:00 PM right after my 12:00 PM class. If I can order on my phone at 12:15 PM and just grab it at 12:40 PM, it changes my whole routine."*

> **P4 (Faculty Staff):** *"I like customizing my lunch—less rice, extra salad. In a loud cafeteria, the server always mishears me. Customizing on a mobile screen is much clearer."*
