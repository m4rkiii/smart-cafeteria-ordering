# User Research Questionnaire & Interview Protocol
## Smart Cafeteria Ordering Mobile Application

**Target Audience:** University Students & Academic/Administrative Staff  
**Sample Size:** Minimum 5 Participants  
**Methodology:** 15-minute semi-structured contextual interviews & survey questionnaire  

---

### Section 1: Demographics & Campus Habits
1. What is your role on campus?
   - [ ] Undergraduate Student
   - [ ] Postgraduate Student
   - [ ] Teaching Staff / Faculty
   - [ ] Administrative Staff
2. How often do you eat at campus cafeterias during a typical week?
   - [ ] 1–2 times/week
   - [ ] 3–4 times/week
   - [ ] 5+ times/week (Daily)
3. Which time window do you usually visit the cafeteria?
   - [ ] Breakfast (07:30 – 09:00 AM)
   - [ ] Peak Lunch (11:30 AM – 01:30 PM)
   - [ ] Late Lunch (01:30 – 03:00 PM)
   - [ ] Evening Snack / Dinner (04:00 – 07:00 PM)

---

### Section 2: Pain Points & Queue Experience
4. On average, how long do you spend in line to order and receive food during peak lunch hours?
   - [ ] Less than 10 minutes
   - [ ] 10–20 minutes
   - [ ] 20–35 minutes
   - [ ] Over 35 minutes
5. Have you ever abandoned a line or skipped lunch because of long queues?
   - [ ] Frequently
   - [ ] Occasionally
   - [ ] Rarely / Never
6. What is your single biggest frustration when ordering food in person?
   - [ ] Long physical waiting times
   - [ ] Finding out an item is sold out after queuing
   - [ ] No visibility of kitchen preparation times
   - [ ] Crowded and noisy pickup counters
   - [ ] Rigid menu choices / difficulty customizing

---

### Section 3: Mobile Pre-Ordering Concept Evaluation
7. If a mobile app allowed you to order food 30 minutes ahead and pick it up at a designated express counter, would you use it?
   - [ ] Yes, definitely
   - [ ] Probably
   - [ ] Unsure
   - [ ] No
8. Which payment method would you prefer for quick mobile checkout?
   - [ ] M-Pesa Mobile Money
   - [ ] Campus Student/Staff Smart Card Account
   - [ ] Debit / Credit Card
   - [ ] Pay at Express Collection Counter
9. How important is the ability to schedule a specific collection time (e.g. 12:45 PM)?
   - [ ] Extremely Important
   - [ ] Moderately Important
   - [ ] Not Important
10. What features would build your trust in a cafeteria ordering app? *(Select all that apply)*
    - [ ] Real-time status updates (*Preparing* -> *Ready*)
    - [ ] Instant refund / clear cancellation policy
    - [ ] Accurate stock availability indicators (*Sold Out* tags)
    - [ ] Itemized digital receipts
