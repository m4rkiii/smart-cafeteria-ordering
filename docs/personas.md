# User Personas
## Smart Cafeteria Ordering Mobile Application

To ground our HCI design in empirical user needs, two primary user personas were developed based on early user interviews at the university campus.

---

### Persona 1: University Student (Primary User)

![Alex Chen - Student Persona](https://via.placeholder.com/150/2563eb/ffffff?text=Alex+Chen)

* **Name:** Alex Chen  
* **Age:** 21  
* **Role:** 3rd Year Computer Science Student  
* **Tech Literacy:** High (Daily smartphone user)  
* **Daily Schedule:** Back-to-back lectures with 45-minute lunch breaks between 12:00 PM and 1:00 PM.  

#### Quote
> *"I only have 40 minutes between my lab and my math lecture. If the cafeteria line is out the door, I end up skipping lunch or grabbing a bag of chips."*

#### Goals & Motivations:
- Order a hot, nutritious lunch and collect it within a 5-minute window between classes.
- Avoid standing in 20-minute physical queues during peak 12:30 PM rush hour.
- See exact prices and meal combinations within a tight student budget (under KSh 350).
- Get instant alerts when the meal is cooked and waiting on the collection shelf.

#### Pain Points:
- Arriving at the counter after waiting 15 minutes only to find the *Chicken Combo* is sold out.
- No visibility into kitchen preparation times.
- Congested, noisy pickup counters where staff shout out order numbers.

#### Empathy Map:
- **Thinks:** *"Will I make it to my 1:00 PM lecture on time if I join this line?"*
- **Feels:** Anxious, hurried, hungry, frustrated by slow-moving queues.
- **Sees:** Packed dining halls, long lines snaking out the door, crowded pickup tables.
- **Does:** Searches for fast food options, checks phone constantly, re-orders familiar budget meals.

### Persona 2: University Staff Member (Secondary User)

![Dr. Sarah Jenkins - Staff Persona](https://via.placeholder.com/150/059669/ffffff?text=Dr.+Jenkins)

* **Name:** Dr. Sarah Jenkins  
* **Age:** 38  
* **Role:** Associate Professor of Economics & Department Chair  
* **Tech Literacy:** Moderate (Uses productivity apps & digital calendar)  
* **Daily Schedule:** Packed office hours, back-to-back committee meetings, unpredictable lunch availability.  

#### Quote
> *"My lunch breaks depend on when department meetings end. I need to schedule food pick-up for exactly 1:15 PM so I can eat in my office before student consultations."*

#### Goals & Motivations:
- Schedule meal collection for a specific, non-peak time slot (e.g. 1:15 PM).
- Customize orders (extra salad, no mayo, herbal tea instead of soda).
- Quickly reorder her weekly favorite salad combo without navigating complex menus.
- Receive a digital receipt for departmental expense tracking.

#### Pain Points:
- Unpredictable queue lengths wasting her limited 30-minute break.
- Rigid pre-set menu options that lack customization choices.
- Having to stand in line alongside hundreds of noisy undergraduates.

#### Empathy Map:
- **Thinks:** *"I need a healthy meal scheduled precisely for my break window."*
- **Feels:** Time-pressured, overwhelmed by peak cafeteria noise, values efficiency.
- **Sees:** Busy counter staff, chaotic pickup areas, unorganized lines.
- **Does:** Plans meals hours ahead, values reliable notifications and quiet pickup points.
