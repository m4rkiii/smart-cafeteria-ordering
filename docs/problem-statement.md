# Problem Statement
## Smart Cafeteria Ordering Mobile Application

### 1. Context and Campus Background
University cafeterias serve thousands of students, faculty, and administrative staff daily. During peak hours—specifically breakfast (07:30 - 09:00 AM) and lunch (11:30 AM - 02:00 PM)—demand spikes drastically. Most campus dining venues operate on traditional counter-ordering models where customers queue up to view physical menus, place verbal orders, make payments, wait for preparation, and collect food.

---

### 2. Identified Bottlenecks & Friction Points

```
[Arrival] ──> [Queue to View Menu & Order] ──> [Queue to Pay] ──> [Wait for Kitchen Prep] ──> [Queue to Collect]
               (10 - 15 mins)                  (5 - 10 mins)       (10 - 20 mins)             (5 mins)
```
*Total average time spent in cafeteria: 30 to 50 minutes per lunch break.*

#### Key Pain Points:
1. **Excessive Physical Waiting:** Students with tight 45-minute gaps between lectures spend up to 40 minutes standing in line, frequently leading to missed meals or tardiness to classes.
2. **Poor Menu Visibility:** Daily specials and availability (e.g. sold-out items) are only visible upon reaching the cash counter, leading to decision paralysis at the front of the queue and unexpected disappointment.
3. **Lack of Order Status Visibility:** Once payment is made, customers wait blindly in crowded pick-up areas without knowing whether their order is being cooked or ready.
4. **Cafeteria Congestion & Safety:** High customer density during peak windows creates noise, physical overcrowding, seating shortages, and operational chaos for kitchen staff.
5. **Inflexible Dining Choices for Staff:** University staff members with strict meeting schedules avoid dining halls altogether due to unpredictable service speeds.

---

### 3. The Proposed HCI Solution
The **Smart Cafeteria Ordering Mobile Application** shifts the dining paradigm from *in-person queueing* to *asynchronous remote pre-ordering*:

```
[Remote Browse & Customize on Mobile] ──> [Select Scheduled Pickup Slot] ──> [Mobile Payment] ──> [Track Status Remotely] ──> [Express Pickup]
 (2 mins anywhere on campus)               (e.g., 12:30 PM slot)            (Instant)          (Live Stepper)             (< 1 min wait)
```

By decoupling the ordering/payment steps from physical cafeteria presence, the system guarantees:
- Reduced queue lengths by up to 50%.
- Real-time stock transparency (Sold Out items disabled).
- Precise collection scheduling matched to student class breaks.
- Visual order progress transparency (*Received* → *Preparing* → *Ready*).
