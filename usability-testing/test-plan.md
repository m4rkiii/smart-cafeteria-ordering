# Usability Testing Plan & Protocol
## Smart Cafeteria Ordering Mobile Application

**Test Objective:** Evaluate the usability, navigation clarity, task completion rates, and HCI heuristic compliance of the Smart Cafeteria Ordering Mobile Application prototype across 5 representative campus users.

---

### 1. Test Methodology & Metrics
- **Format:** Moderated, 1-on-1 usability testing sessions (in-person or screen-share).
- **Duration:** 25 minutes per participant.
- **Metrics Collected:**
  - **Task Completion Rate (TCR):** % of tasks completed without critical errors.
  - **Time on Task (TOT):** Duration in seconds from task start to completion.
  - **Error Rate:** Count of mis-taps, wrong navigation paths, or confusion points.
  - **System Usability Scale (SUS):** Standardized 10-question usability score (0-100).
  - **Qualitative Feedback:** User comments, facial expressions, and friction points.

---

### 2. Test Tasks (Realistic Scenarios)

#### Task 1: Find a Meal & Place an Order (Scenario 1)
* **Instruction:** "You have 30 minutes before your next lecture. Open the app, find the *Grilled Chicken & Rice*, customize it to a Large portion with extra sauce, select a 12:30 PM pickup slot at the Main Cafeteria, and pay using M-Pesa."
* **Success Criteria:** Reaches Order Confirmation screen with correct customization, venue, and time.

#### Task 2: Track Active Order Status (Scenario 2)
* **Instruction:** "You placed an order 10 minutes ago. Check how your food preparation is progressing from the Home screen."
* **Success Criteria:** Locates Active Order card on Home screen or Orders tab, views the 4-stage tracking stepper.

#### Task 3: Reorder a Past Favorite (Scenario 3)
* **Instruction:** "You want to reorder the exact same lunch you bought yesterday. Locate your order history and place a reorder in as few taps as possible."
* **Success Criteria:** Navigates to Order History or Quick Reorder tray and completes checkout in under 4 taps.

#### Task 4: Save a Meal to Favorites
* **Instruction:** "Browse the menu, find the *Fresh Mango Juice*, and bookmark it to your Favorites so you can find it quickly tomorrow."
* **Success Criteria:** Taps heart icon on card or details screen and verifies item appears in Favorites tab.

#### Task 5: Handle a Sold-Out Item (Error Prevention Check)
* **Instruction:** "Try to add the *Beef Burger* to your cart."
* **Success Criteria:** Recognizes the "Sold Out" red badge and notices the disabled "Add to Cart" button without attempting invalid checkout.
