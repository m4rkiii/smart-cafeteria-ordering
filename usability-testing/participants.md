# Usability Test Participants
## Smart Cafeteria Ordering Mobile Application

Five representative users participated in the usability testing evaluation for Prototype Version 1 and Version 2.

---

### Participant Demographics & Background Matrix

| Participant ID | Demographic Category | Age | Major / Department | Smartphone Usage | Prior Mobile Food Ordering Experience |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | Undergraduate Student | 20 | Computer Science (Yr 2) | High (Daily iOS user) | High (UberEats, Glovo) |
| **P2** | Undergraduate Student | 22 | Civil Engineering (Yr 4) | High (Daily Android user) | Moderate (Occasional ordering) |
| **P3** | Postgraduate Student | 26 | MBA (Master's Student) | High (Daily Android user) | High (Frequent food apps) |
| **P4** | Academic Staff | 41 | Senior Lecturer, Law | Moderate (Daily iOS user) | Low (Prefers in-person dining) |
| **P5** | University Admin | 33 | Finance Department | Moderate (Daily Android user) | Moderate (Occasional ordering) |

---

### Key Pre-Test Expectations
- **P1 & P3 (Frequent App Users):** Expected fluid gesture navigation, sticky bottom bars, instant search filters, and single-tap reorder options.
- **P4 (Low Food App Experience):** Needed explicit button labels (e.g. "Select Pickup Time" instead of generic icons) and large, clear touch targets.
- **P2 & P5:** Emphasized the importance of seeing exact collection time availability before committing to payment.
