# Group Presentation Slide Deck Outline
## Smart Cafeteria Ordering Mobile Application

---

### Slide 1: Title & Team Introduction
* **Title:** Smart Cafeteria Ordering Mobile Application
* **Subtitle:** HCI Group Term Assignment
* **Core Value Proposition:** *"Order ahead, skip the queue, and collect your meal when it's ready."*
* **Team Members & Roles:** Project Lead, UX Researcher, Balsamiq Designers, Usability Testing Lead, GitHub Manager.

---

### Slide 2: The Campus Queue Problem
* **Context:** Peak lunch rush (11:30 AM – 1:30 PM) creates 25+ minute queues.
* **Pain Points:** Missed lectures, skipped meals, noise congestion, sold-out item disappointment.
* **Impact:** 80% of surveyed students report queue friction weekly.

---

### Slide 3: Target Personas
* **Alex Chen (21, Student):** Tight 45-minute lunch break, budget-conscious, wants rapid express pickup.
* **Dr. Sarah Jenkins (38, Faculty Staff):** Needs scheduled 1:15 PM pickup slot to align with office hours.

---

### Slide 4: Interactive Wireframe Overview (15 Screens)
* Demonstration of Balsamiq wireframes across 5 bottom nav tabs: `Home | Menu | Orders | Favorites | Profile`.
* Highlight of core interaction scenarios: *Order Placement*, *Order Tracking*, *1-Tap Reordering*.

---

### Slide 5: Usability Testing Results (V1 vs V2)
* Tested with 5 representative campus users.
* **SUS Usability Score:** Increased from **68.5 (V1)** to **86.5 (V2)**.
* **Average Task Completion Time:** Reduced from 84 seconds to 48 seconds (43% speed improvement).

---

### Slide 6: Key Iterations Driven by Usability Data
* Added **Active Order Card** directly to Home Dashboard for immediate status visibility.
* Separated **Collection Time Selection** into a dedicated mandatory step to prevent payment errors.
* Added 1-tap **Express Reorder** button.

---

### Slide 7: Live Prototype Demonstration & Conclusion
* Live walkthrough of the interactive prototype.
* Summary of key HCI insights and future implementation roadmap (M-Pesa integration, staff kitchen dashboard).
