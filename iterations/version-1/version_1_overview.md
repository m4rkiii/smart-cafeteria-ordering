# Prototype Version 1 Overview (Initial Balsamiq Wireframe)
## Smart Cafeteria Ordering Mobile Application

Version 1 represents the initial digital wireframe prototype designed prior to formal usability testing.

---

### Key Architectural Characteristics of Version 1
1. **Home Screen Layout:** Included Search, Category Pills, Promotions, and Recommended Meals.
   - *Friction Point:* Active order status was not visible on the Home screen.
2. **Checkout Flow:** Combined Collection Time, Venue, and Payment into a single long scrolling screen.
   - *Friction Point:* Users frequently scrolled past the collection time dropdown and attempted to pay without selecting a pickup slot.
3. **Reordering Experience:** Tapping "Reorder" in Order History opened the Meal Details page instead of immediately populating the cart.
   - *Friction Point:* Required 3 extra taps to complete a reorder.
4. **Sold-Out Items:** Sold-out meal cards allowed users to open the details modal before displaying a warning toast.
   - *Friction Point:* Annoyed users who spent time customizing an unavailable meal.

---

### Initial Usability Metrics (V1)
- **Task Completion Rate:** 76% across 5 core tasks.
- **Average Order Time:** 84 seconds.
- **System Usability Scale (SUS) Score:** 68.5 / 100.
